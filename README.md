## About the Simulation

Counter-Manager is an HTML5 management game set in the competitive CS:GO and CS2 environment. Start with a custom club, build its lineup, place players into a five-role structure, tune their attributes, and lead the organization through matches and important tournament rounds.

A campaign covers more than match results. You will work with a round-by-round economy, monitor a live kill feed, negotiate through the transfer market, oversee club finances, and follow HLTV-inspired global rankings. Browser LocalStorage preserves campaign progress so a management career can be resumed later.

---

## Core Features

- Establish and name your own esports organization.
- Build and maintain a roster using five player roles.
- Inspect and modify individual player attributes.
- Handle the economy decisions that take place across CS rounds.
- Watch match events through a live kill feed system.
- Search the transfer market and manage player moves.
- Oversee the club's financial position.
- Take part in major tournament progression.
- Compare teams through HLTV-style world rankings.
- Keep campaign saves in browser LocalStorage.
- Run directly in a browser using HTML5 and Tailwind CSS.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the quickest way to launch the game?

Open the [download link](https://chrisqrvfreed3582.github.io/counter-manager-tournament-hub/), then create an organization and begin setting up its roster.

### Where does Counter-Manager keep save data?

Campaigns are saved in browser LocalStorage. To preserve a local career, continue using the same browser profile and do not clear the site's stored data.

### What parts of a team can I customize?

You can create a custom esports club, manage its roster, assign roles, and adjust player attribute values.

### Are matches simulated?

Yes. Match play includes a CS round economy system together with a live kill feed engine.

### Is there a transfer system?

Yes. The player transfer market lets you handle player movement while developing and managing your club.

### Does the simulation include tournaments and rankings?

Major tournament progression is available, and HLTV world rankings provide a view of the wider competitive field.

### How do I get the newest version?

Open the latest build link to use the currently published release. Updates can change game systems, rankings, or how existing campaign data behaves.

### Can a save be transferred to another browser?

LocalStorage data belongs to the browser environment in which it was created. Moving a save depends on browser-specific export or data-transfer tools, if those tools are available.
